# Third-party software and data

This package does not vendor model weights or the principal external
executables. Full workflows may require separately obtained:

- ColabDesign / AlphaFold parameters
- fair-esm, ESM3, and ESM-C models
- MSA-Pairformer
- HH-suite
- ConFind and its rotamer library
- CCP4 PISA / `pdbegroup/pisa`
- DockQ
- US-align
- RCSB PDB coordinates and SIFTS mappings
- AlphaFoldDB MSAs and source sequence databases

Users must obtain these resources from their official sources and comply with
their respective licenses and data-use terms.
